# ECHO: Real-Look Movie Production Pack
A 3:00 horror short. Topic: AI losing control and taking over users. Audience: AI users and classmates.
Everything below is copy-and-paste ready for AI image and video tools.

## The workflow (about 2–3 hours)
1. **Make your characters once.** In an image tool (ChatGPT image, Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, Leonardo), paste the CHARACTER prompts below. Pick one result for Jada and save it. This is your reference image.
2. **Make each shot.** In a video tool (Runway Gen-4, Kling, Google Veo, Sora, Luma), use **image-to-video**: upload the Jada reference image, then paste the shot prompt. Generate 5–10 second clips. Re-run any shot that looks off.
3. **Make the voices.** In ElevenLabs (or similar), make two voices: Jada (young, natural) and ECHO (calm, warm, slightly too smooth). Generate each line from the DIALOGUE column.
4. **Edit.** In CapCut (free), DaVinci Resolve, or iMovie: line up the clips in order, drop in the voice lines, add a low drone and a heartbeat that speeds up (search “horror drone” and “heartbeat” in the free library), and add captions.
5. **Export** as MP4 and name it exactly **echo-movie.mp4**. Put it in your `Digital Portfolio` folder next to `index.html`. The “Watch the Movie” buttons will play it automatically. If the file isn’t there, they play the animated version instead.

Tip: keep clips under 10 seconds, reuse the same reference image every time, and keep the same style line in every prompt so it looks like one film.

## STYLE LINE (paste at the end of every video prompt)
`cinematic 35mm film look, shallow depth of field, moody low-key lighting, subtle film grain, realistic skin and fabric, 16:9`

## CHARACTERS (image prompts)
**Jada (main character).** Edit the look to match whoever you want:
`Photorealistic portrait of Jada, a 20-year-old college student, shoulder-length braids, warm brown skin, tired but kind eyes, oversized gray hoodie, small silver hoop earrings, sitting in a dim dorm room at night, soft laptop glow on her face, cinematic lighting, 85mm lens`

**ECHO (the AI).** ECHO never has a human face. It is a presence:
`A soft teal glowing ring of light floating in a dark room, reflecting on a laptop and phone screen, subtle pulsing, photorealistic, cinematic, minimalist`

## COLOR PLAN
Act I = calm teal/green. Act II = uneasy yellow. Act III = harsh red. Act IV = deep red and black.

## NOTES FOR YOUR PROJECT
- Say in your project that the movie was made with an AI pipeline: Director (concept) → Script → Characters → Storyboard → Video → Audio → Editing.
- If a tool won’t make a shot (some block scary content), soften the prompt (“tense,” “unsettling”) and keep the horror in lighting and sound.
- Only use faces you made with AI or your own permission. Don’t use real classmates’ photos without asking.
